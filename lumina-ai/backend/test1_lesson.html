Objectives
By the end of the lesson, the students will be able to: 1. Identify and name different shapes (square, circle, triangle, rectangle) 2. Recognize and describe the characteristics of each shape
Special Notes: MATATAG Curriculum, Grade 1, Quarter 1, 50 minutes, Intermediate difficulty
Motivation
Teacher will show the students pictures of different shapes and ask if they know the names of the shapes. Students will be encouraged to share their knowledge and experiences with shapes.
Special Notes: none
Lesson Proper
Introduction (5 minutes) Teacher will review the shapes learned in previous lessons. Students will be asked to share what they know about shapes. Teacher will write the names of the shapes on the board.
Direct Instruction (15 minutes) Teacher will show pictures of different shapes and describe their characteristics. Students will be asked to identify and name the shapes. Teacher will provide examples and non-examples of each shape.
Guided Practice (15 minutes) Teacher will give students worksheets with pictures of different shapes. Students will be asked to identify and name the shapes. Teacher will circulate around the room to assist students.
Independent Practice (10 minutes) Students will be given a set of shapes to identify and name. Students will work individually to complete the task. Teacher will monitor students' progress.
Conclusion (5 minutes) Teacher will review the shapes learned in the lesson. Students will be asked to share what they learned. Teacher will provide feedback and encouragement.
Special Notes: none
Application
Students will be given a real-life scenario where they need to identify and name shapes. Students will work in groups to solve the problem. Teacher will provide guidance and support as needed.
Special Notes: none
Evaluation
Teacher will observe students during the guided and independent practice activities. Teacher will review students' worksheets and assignments. Teacher will use a rubric to assess students' understanding of shapes.
Special Notes: none
Assignment
Students will be given a homework assignment to identify and name shapes in their daily lives. Students will be asked to draw and label a picture of a shape. Students will be encouraged to share their findings with the class.
Special Notes: due next class
Remarks
Students will be encouraged to ask questions and seek help when needed. Teacher will provide additional support and resources as needed. Students will be reminded to practice identifying and naming shapes regularly.
Special Notes: none
Reflection
Teacher will reflect on the effectiveness of the lesson. Teacher will identify areas for improvement. Teacher will plan for future lessons to reinforce students' understanding of shapes.
