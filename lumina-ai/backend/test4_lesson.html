Objectives
By the end of the lesson, the students will be able to:
  • Identify and classify different types of nouns (common, proper, collective).
  • Use nouns correctly in sentences.
Motivation
Teacher will ask students:

What are some things we can find in our classroom?
Students will respond:

Desks, chairs, books, etc.
Teacher will ask:

Can you tell me what kind of words those are?
Students will respond:

They are nouns.
Lesson Proper
Introduction (5 minutes) Teacher will review the concept of nouns. Students will participate in a class discussion.
Direct Instruction (15 minutes) Teacher will explain the different types of nouns (common, proper, collective). Students will take notes.
Guided Practice (15 minutes) Teacher will provide examples of nouns and ask students to identify the type. Students will work in pairs.
Independent Practice (10 minutes) Teacher will provide a worksheet with sentences and ask students to identify the nouns. Students will work individually.
Conclusion (5 minutes) Teacher will review the objectives and ask students to share what they learned. Students will participate in a class discussion.
Application
Teacher will ask students to:

Write a short paragraph using at least three different types of nouns.
Students will respond:

Example: The city of Manila is a collective of people from different countries.

Evaluation
Teacher will use the following criteria to evaluate student performance:
  • Accuracy in identifying and classifying nouns.
  • Correct use of nouns in sentences.
Assignment
Teacher will assign the following task:

Write a short story using at least five different types of nouns.
Remarks
Teacher will provide feedback on student performance. Students will be encouraged to ask questions and seek help when needed.
Reflection
Teacher will reflect on the effectiveness of the lesson. Students will reflect on what they learned and how they can apply it in their daily lives.
