Objectives
Students will be able to demonstrate proper breathing techniques while running. Students will be able to identify the importance of breathing techniques in running.
Motivation
Good morning, class! Today we're going to learn about running and how to breathe properly while doing it. Who here loves to run? Student 1: I love running! Student 2: Me too!
Lesson Proper
1. Introduction (5 minutes): Explain the importance of breathing techniques in running. Teacher: When we run, we need to breathe in and out properly to get enough oxygen to our muscles. This helps us run faster and longer.
2. Demonstration (10 minutes): Demonstrate proper breathing techniques while running. Teacher: Watch how I breathe in and out while running. (demonstrates proper breathing techniques)
3. Guided Practice (15 minutes): Have students practice proper breathing techniques while running. Teacher: Now it's your turn! Run around the playground and practice your breathing techniques.
4. Independent Practice (10 minutes): Have students run on their own and practice proper breathing techniques. Teacher: Keep running and remember to breathe properly!
5. Conclusion (10 minutes): Review the importance of breathing techniques in running. Teacher: Remember, proper breathing techniques are important for running. Let's practice it every time we run!
Application
Students will apply their knowledge of proper breathing techniques in running by practicing it during recess or extracurricular activities. Student 1: I'll practice my breathing techniques during recess. Student 2: Me too!
Evaluation
Observe students during recess or extracurricular activities to assess their ability to apply proper breathing techniques in running. Teacher: I observed Student 1 and Student 2 during recess. They both demonstrated proper breathing techniques while running!
Assignment
Have students draw a picture of themselves running and labeling the different parts of their body that they use to breathe properly. Student 1: I drew a picture of myself running and labeled my lungs and diaphragm. Student 2: Me too!
Remarks
Remember to practice proper breathing techniques every time you run. Teacher: Remember, proper breathing techniques are important for running. Keep practicing!
Reflection
What did you learn about proper breathing techniques in running? Student 1: I learned that I need to breathe in and out properly to get enough oxygen to my muscles. Student 2: Me too!
