Objectives
By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:
  • Explain the importance of business planning in entrepreneurship.
  • Identify the key components of a business plan.
  • Develop a simple business plan for a small-scale enterprise.
Motivation
Why is business planning important in entrepreneurship?

A business plan is a roadmap that guides entrepreneurs in making informed decisions and achieving their goals. It helps to identify potential risks and opportunities, and to allocate resources effectively.
Lesson Proper
Introduction (5 minutes) Present the importance of business planning in entrepreneurship.

Teacher: "A business plan is essential for entrepreneurs to achieve their goals and make informed decisions. It helps to identify potential risks and opportunities, and to allocate resources effectively."
Direct Instruction (15 minutes) Explain the key components of a business plan, including executive summary, market analysis, product or service description, marketing and sales strategy, financial plan, and management and organization.

Teacher: "The executive summary is a brief overview of the business plan, highlighting the key points and goals. The market analysis identifies the target market and competition. The product or service description explains the features and benefits of the product or service."
Guided Practice (15 minutes) Have students work in groups to develop a simple business plan for a small-scale enterprise.

Student 1: "Our business plan will focus on a small-scale food cart that sells Filipino dishes. We will target students and office workers in the area."

Student 2: "We will offer a variety of dishes, including adobo, sinigang, and lechon kawali. We will also offer a loyalty program to encourage repeat customers."
Independent Practice (10 minutes) Have students present their business plans to the class.

Student 3: "Our business plan is to start a small-scale online business selling Filipino crafts. We will target tourists and locals who are interested in Filipino culture."

Student 4: "We will offer a variety of crafts, including t-shirts, bags, and home decor items. We will also offer a delivery service to make it more convenient for customers."
Application
How can you apply the concepts learned in this lesson to your own business or entrepreneurial venture?

Students will reflect on how they can apply the concepts learned in this lesson to their own business or entrepreneurial venture. They will think about how they can create a business plan that is tailored to their specific needs and goals.
Evaluation
What are the key components of a business plan?

Students will be evaluated on their understanding of the key components of a business plan. They will be asked to identify and explain the different components of a business plan.
Assignment
Develop a simple business plan for a small-scale enterprise.

Students will be assigned to develop a simple business plan for a small-scale enterprise. They will be asked to identify the target market, competition, product or service, marketing and sales strategy, financial plan, and management and organization.
Remarks
Remember to include the following in your business plan:
  • Executive summary
  • Market analysis
  • Product or service description
  • Marketing and sales strategy
  • Financial plan
  • Management and organization
Reflection
What did you learn from this lesson?

Students will reflect on what they learned from this lesson. They will think about how they can apply the concepts learned in this lesson to their own business or entrepreneurial venture.
